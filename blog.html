Impact of technology on watch

Technology has added very positive impact on watch industry and consumer trends. Watch now a days just not ony tell time but it also works and count as electronic device. Consumer now value multifunctional devices that go beyond just telling time. Main role here is played by addition of smart features to watches,and watches having smart feature in it is known as smartwatch. Smartwatch offers features like Fitness and Health tracking which includes heart rate monitoring , step count , calories burn etc.Connectivity features,Voice assistance,Music and Media control,Battery and charging , Notifications and alert features are example of smart features in smartwatch.Smartwatch has also change the pattern of how people buy watch.People now a days are influenced by technology advancement which they finds in smartwatch that help them stay healthy,show notifications and do more tha just tell time. It is belived that smart watches have make life easier.
